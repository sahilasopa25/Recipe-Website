Gulab Jamun

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 2-3 tbsp milk (to bind dough)
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 4-5 cardamom pods, crushed
  • Oil or ghee, for frying

Recipe

  1. Mix milk powder, flour, and baking soda in a bowl
  2. Rub in the ghee, then add milk gradually to form a soft, smooth dough
  3. Shape the dough into small, crack-free balls
  4. Heat sugar, water, and cardamom together until syrupy; keep warm
  5. Fry the balls on low-medium heat until evenly golden brown
  6. Drop the hot fried balls into the warm sugar syrup and let them soak for at least 1 hour before serving

Hot Dog

Ingredients:

  • 4 hot dog sausages
  • 4 hot dog buns
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Ketchup and mustard
  • Chopped onions
  • Shredded cheese (optional)

Recipe

  1. Boil or pan-fry the sausages until heated through and lightly browned
  2. Butter the buns lightly and toast them on a pan until golden
  3. Place a sausage in each bun
  4. Top with ketchup, mustard, chopped onions, and cheese as desired
  5. Serve warm immediately

Cotton Candy

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • Few drops of food coloring (pink or your choice)
  • 1/4 tsp flavoring extract (optional, e.g. vanilla or strawberry)

Recipe

  1. Mix the sugar with food coloring and flavoring until evenly tinted
  2. Pour the colored sugar into a cotton candy machine (or heat gently in a pan if making by hand)
  3. Let the sugar melt and spin into fine threads
  4. Gather the threads onto a stick or cone using a twirling motion
  5. Serve immediately, as cotton candy dissolves quickly in humidity
